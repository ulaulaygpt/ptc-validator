What is a PTC declaration?

A file that verifiably declares whether an AI system was involved in a document, a file, or a reference, and how. It is cryptographically linked to what it declares: if the file changes, the declaration no longer matches.

Privacy and local processing

The file never leaves your device. All computation — reading, fingerprints, package generation — happens in your browser. Validator does not upload anything to any server.

Reference an official document

Describe the AI’s participation

This is the text that will appear in the PTC.

State the platform or provider and, when known, the model or version. You may declare several systems, separated by semicolons.

The level describes the intensity of AI participation. It does not change the PTC’s cryptographic validity or the result of its verification. It is not a scale of quality or prestige.

Basic — limited assistance

AI was used for specific, delimited tasks such as correction, translation, summarisation, consultation, reformulation or targeted technical support.

Standard — operator-led co-creation

The process involved dialogue, iterations or joint construction. AI made a relevant contribution, while the operator directed the process, made the substantive decisions and assumed final responsibility.

Extended — intensive co-creation

AI participation was sustained or complex and the process can be described in some detail: how it developed, which systems took part and which relevant decisions were made.

Other — case not covered

Use this option when the process does not fit the preceding levels. Selecting it opens a dedicated field to describe the case.
