Why Validator exists →

Validator.es creates verifiable declarations about the participation of artificial intelligence in documents. Each declaration is cryptographically bound to the content: if the document or declaration changes, verification detects it.

Validator verifies document integrity and the declaration’s technical conformance with the PTC protocol. The result can support transparency, audit and compliance processes; it does not replace legal assessment of the specific case.
